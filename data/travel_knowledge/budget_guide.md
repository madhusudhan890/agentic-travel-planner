# Travel Budget Guide — Comprehensive Cost Reference

## Budget Categories Explained

### Backpacker / Budget (< $50/day per person)
- Stay in hostels, guesthouses ($10-25/night)
- Eat street food and local restaurants ($5-15/day)
- Use public transport exclusively
- Focus on free attractions (parks, markets, temples)
- Best destinations: Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, India, Central America

### Mid-Range ($50-150/day per person)
- 3-star hotels or good guesthouses ($40-100/night)
- Mix of local restaurants and occasional nicer dining ($20-40/day)
- Occasional taxis and private transport
- Entrance fees to major attractions
- Best for: Most of Asia, Southern Europe, Latin America

### Comfortable Traveler ($150-300/day per person)
- 4-star hotels ($100-200/night)
- Restaurant dining for most meals ($40-80/day)
- Private transport, guided tours
- Premium experiences (cooking classes, sunset cruises)
- Works well: Western Europe, Japan, USA, Australia

### Luxury ($300+/day per person)
- 5-star hotels and boutique properties ($250+/night)
- Fine dining experiences ($80+/day)
- Private drivers, exclusive experiences
- Business class flights, airport lounges

## Budget Allocation Formula

For a trip with total budget B, num_travelers T, and num_days D:

**Recommended split (international trip):**
- Flights: 20-35% of budget
- Accommodation: 25-35% of budget
- Food: 15-25% of budget
- Activities/experiences: 10-15% of budget
- Local transport: 5-10% of budget
- Shopping/miscellaneous: 5-10% of budget
- Emergency buffer: 10-15% MINIMUM

**Daily ground budget** = (Total - Flights) / Days / Travelers

## Money-Saving Strategies

### Flights
- Book 6-8 weeks ahead for international, 4-6 weeks for domestic
- Be flexible ±3 days — can save 30-50%
- Use Google Flights price calendar
- Consider budget airlines: AirAsia (Asia), Ryanair (Europe), Spirit (USA)
- Fly into secondary airports: Osaka instead of Tokyo, Pisa instead of Florence

### Accommodation
- Mix hostels (dorm beds) with private rooms — huge savings
- Airbnb for groups of 3+ often beats hotels
- Book Monday-Thursday — weekend rates higher
- City center vs 1-2 metro stops out — 40% cheaper

### Food
- Lunch > dinner: same restaurant, same quality, 30-40% cheaper
- Street food: often better and cheaper than tourist restaurants
- Cook breakfast at hostel/Airbnb
- Happy hour: drinks 5-7pm half price at many restaurants
- Local vs tourist areas: same dish, 3x different price

### Activities
- Free walking tours (tip-based)
- Museum free days (most museums have 1 day/month free)
- City passes: often include transport + top attractions
- Student/youth discounts: always ask
- Book activities online ahead: often 10-20% cheaper than at door

## Currency Exchange — Best Practices

1. **Never exchange at airports** — rates are 8-15% worse
2. **Use local ATMs** — generally best rates (use bank ATMs, not airport/tourist kiosks)
3. **Avoid Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC)** — when ATM asks "charge in local currency or your home currency?" — ALWAYS choose local currency
4. **Notify your bank** before international travel to prevent card blocks
5. **Carry 2 cards** — one as backup if primary is lost/frozen
6. **Wise or Revolut** (for Europeans): Best exchange rates with no foreign transaction fees
7. **Cash**: Carry enough for first 24 hours in case ATM issues
