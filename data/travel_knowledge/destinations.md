# Popular Travel Destinations Guide

## Japan — Tokyo & Kyoto

### Overview
Japan is a country in East Asia with a population of approximately 125 million. It is known for its unique blend of ancient traditions and ultra-modern technology. Japan ranks among the safest countries in the world for travelers.

### Best Time to Visit
- **Spring (March-May)**: Cherry blossom season — most popular and magical time
- **Autumn (October-November)**: Fall foliage — stunning red and gold leaves
- **Winter (December-February)**: Fewer crowds, snow in Hokkaido, illuminations
- **Summer (June-August)**: Hot and humid, typhoon season, but festivals abound
- **Avoid Golden Week** (late April to early May) — everything is crowded and expensive

### Tokyo — Key Neighborhoods
- **Shibuya**: World's busiest crossing, youth fashion, nightlife, Harajuku nearby
- **Shinjuku**: Business hub, Kabukicho entertainment, Golden Gai bar alley, Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building (free observation deck)
- **Asakusa**: Traditional Tokyo — Senso-ji temple, rickshaws, craft shops
- **Akihabara**: Electronics, anime, manga culture
- **Ginza**: Luxury shopping, high-end restaurants
- **Odaiba**: Waterfront, teamLab digital art, shopping

### Kyoto — Must-See
- **Fushimi Inari Shrine**: 10,000 torii gates, hike takes 2-3 hours to summit
- **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove**: Best visited before 8am to avoid crowds
- **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)**: Most photographed temple in Japan
- **Gion District**: Historic geisha district, traditional machiya townhouses
- **Nishiki Market**: "Kyoto's Kitchen" — 400-year-old covered market, 100+ stalls

### Transportation
- **JR Pass**: Unlimited shinkansen (bullet train) — Worth it for 2+ weeks
- **IC Card (Suica/Pasmo)**: Rechargeable card for all trains, buses, convenience stores
- **Bullet trains**: Tokyo ↔ Kyoto takes 2h 20min (₹3,000 / $20 without pass)
- **Within cities**: Subway is fastest, buses for scenic areas

### Food — Must Try
- **Ramen**: Regional varieties — Tokyo shoyu, Sapporo miso, Fukuoka tonkotsu
- **Sushi**: Conveyor belt (kaitenzushi) from ¥100/plate, high-end omakase ¥30,000+
- **Tempura**: Lightly battered seafood and vegetables
- **Yakitori**: Grilled chicken skewers at izakayas
- **Matcha everything**: Ice cream, kit kats, lattes — especially in Kyoto
- **Street food in Asakusa**: Ningyo-yaki (sweet cakes), melonpan

### Practical Information
- **Currency**: Japanese Yen (JPY). ¥1 ≈ $0.007
- **Tipping**: Not practiced — considered rude in restaurants and hotels
- **Wi-Fi**: Pocket Wi-Fi rental at airports (around ¥300/day), recommended
- **Cash**: Japan is still very cash-heavy — always carry ¥10,000 minimum
- **Convenience stores (7-Eleven, FamilyMart)**: Excellent food, ATMs, everything
- **Safety**: Extremely safe — can leave belongings unattended at cafes
- **Toilets**: High-tech heated toilet seats everywhere — a unique experience

## Thailand — Bangkok & Bali

### Bangkok Overview
Thailand's capital is a vibrant megacity of 10 million with ancient temples, street food paradise, nightlife, and modern shopping malls existing side by side.

### Must-Visit
- **Grand Palace & Wat Phrakaew**: Most visited site — arrive early, dress respectfully
- **Wat Pho**: Reclining Buddha, also famous for traditional Thai massage
- **Chatuchak Weekend Market**: 15,000 stalls — clothes, antiques, food, everything
- **Khao San Road**: Backpacker hub, budget accommodation, nightlife
- **Asok/Sukhumvit**: Modern Bangkok, Sky Train, rooftop bars, international restaurants
- **Floating Markets** (Damnoen Saduak): 1 hour from Bangkok, touristy but photogenic

### Thailand Food Culture
- **Pad Thai**: National dish, ¥50-100 ($1.50-3) from street stalls
- **Tom Yum soup**: Spicy lemongrass prawn soup
- **Mango sticky rice**: The definitive Thai dessert
- **Som Tam**: Green papaya salad, fiery and fresh
- **Night Markets**: Rod Fai Ratchada, Jodd Fairs — food + entertainment

### Bali, Indonesia
- **Ubud**: Cultural heart — rice terraces, monkey forest, traditional crafts
- **Seminyak**: Upscale beach, sunset cocktails, boutique hotels
- **Canggu**: Digital nomad hub, surf culture, healthy cafes
- **Tanah Lot**: Temple on a rock in the sea — iconic sunset view
- **Tegallalang Rice Terraces**: UNESCO-listed, best light early morning

## France — Paris

### Paris Overview
The City of Light receives 30+ million visitors annually. Rich in art, history, fashion, and gastronomy.

### Neighborhoods
- **Le Marais (3rd-4th arrondissement)**: Historic Jewish quarter, boutiques, galleries
- **Saint-Germain-des-Prés (6th)**: Intellectual café culture, Hemingway haunts
- **Montmartre (18th)**: Bohemian hilltop, Sacré-Cœur, artist studios
- **Champs-Élysées (8th)**: Luxury shopping, Arc de Triomphe
- **Latin Quarter (5th)**: University area, affordable bistros, Shakespeare & Company bookshop

### Must-See
- **Eiffel Tower**: Book tickets online weeks ahead. Best views from Trocadéro at dusk
- **Louvre**: 35,000 artworks, plan 3-4 hours minimum. Buy tickets online
- **Musée d'Orsay**: Impressionist masterpieces — Monet, Renoir, Van Gogh
- **Notre-Dame**: Currently under restoration, exterior viewing still impressive
- **Versailles**: Day trip 40 minutes from Paris, book tickets 2+ weeks ahead

### French Food
- **Boulangeries**: Croissants (€1.20), baguettes (€1.30), pain au chocolat
- **Café culture**: Espresso standing at bar (€1.80) vs sitting (€3.50)
- **Bistros**: Steak-frites €18-25, croque monsieur €10-14
- **Wine**: House red/white carafe from €5 in local bistros
- **Fromages**: 400+ cheese varieties — try a fromagerie market stall

## India — Rajasthan & Goa

### Rajasthan Overview
India's largest state is a desert kingdom of maharajas, forts, camels, and vibrant colors. Best experienced October-March.

### Key Destinations
- **Jaipur (Pink City)**: Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, textile shopping
- **Jodhpur (Blue City)**: Mehrangarh Fort (one of India's largest), narrow blue lanes
- **Udaipur (Lake City)**: City Palace on Lake Pichola, romantic sunset boat rides
- **Jaisalmer (Golden City)**: Living fort, camel safaris in Thar Desert
- **Pushkar**: Sacred town, Brahma temple, annual camel fair (November)

### Golden Triangle
Delhi → Agra (Taj Mahal) → Jaipur — classic 5-7 day circuit for first-time India visitors.

### Goa
- **North Goa**: Party beaches (Baga, Calangute), shacks, nightlife
- **South Goa**: Quiet, pristine (Palolem, Agonda), luxury resorts
- **Season**: November-March (peak). Avoid June-September (monsoon)
- **Food**: Vindaloo, xacuti, prawn curry, bebinca dessert, feni spirit
